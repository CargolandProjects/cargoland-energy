Health, safety, security & environment

Safety comes before schedule.

Our HSSE approach is built on internationally recognised practice and designed to meet the expectations of international oil companies and operators.

Plan the work

Risk assessment and job hazard analysis before every operation; permit-to-work and safe handling procedures.

Protect people on the move

Journey management for road haulage and personnel transport; trained, competent and fit-for-duty crews.

Stop-work authority

Every employee and contractor has the authority and duty to stop unsafe work, without penalty.

Learn from every event

Incident and near-miss reporting, investigation and shared lessons learned.

Be ready to respond

Emergency preparedness, response planning and drills for every site and operation.

One standard for all

Contractors and partners are held to the same HSSE standards as our own people.

Local content

Nigerian & African content

As a 100% Nigerian-owned company, we are committed to the Nigerian Oil and Gas Industry Content Development Act and to building African capability.

We prioritise local employment, skills development, local procurement and partnerships with competent African businesses, without compromising the safety and quality standards our clients require.
