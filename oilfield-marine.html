Cargoland Oilfield & Marine Logistics Ltd

Onshore, swamp and offshore logistics from Port Harcourt.

The wholly owned oilfield and marine logistics subsidiary of Cargoland Energy, at the centre of Nigeria's oil and gas industry.

About the company

Logistics built for the oilfield.

Cargoland Oilfield & Marine Logistics Ltd (RC 10024167) moves equipment, materials and people for operators, contractors and service companies across the Niger Delta.

Backed by Cargoland Energy and the wider Cargoland Group, we combine freight, customs and haulage experience with a dedicated oilfield and marine focus.

  • Operational base: Port Harcourt, Rivers State
  • 100% owned by Cargoland Energy Nig. Ltd
  • Nigerian Content aligned
Our services

End-to-end oilfield logistics

Onshore logistics

Haulage of rig equipment, tubulars, chemicals and oilfield materials; heavy-lift, low-bed and abnormal-load transport; rig moves.

Customs & freight

Customs clearing, freight forwarding and project cargo by air, sea and road, including temporary importation and re-export.

Warehousing

Laydown yards, pipe yards, storage and materials management, with inventory control and expediting.

Marine & offshore

Swamp, shallow-water and offshore supply logistics; vessel charter coordination through qualified providers; quayside support.

Personnel logistics

Crew change, personnel transport and site mobilisation for onshore and offshore operations.

Procurement & supply

Sourcing and supply of oilfield equipment, spares, consumables and PPE, delivered to site.
