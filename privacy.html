Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Who we are

Cargoland Energy Nig. Ltd (RC 9895503), 2nd Floor, Terminal 2 (MMA2), Murtala Muhammed International Airport, Ikeja, Lagos, Nigeria, is responsible for personal data collected through this website. We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

When you contact us, request a quote or submit a partnership enquiry, we collect the details you give us: your name, company, email address, phone number and message. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations, manage business relationships and meet legal and regulatory obligations. We do not sell your data.

Sharing

We share data only where needed to deliver a service you have asked for (for example with Cargoland Oilfield & Marine Logistics Ltd or a vetted service partner), or where the law requires it.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected and protect it with reasonable technical and organisational measures.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its use. Email info@cargolandenergy.com and we will respond within the period set by law.
